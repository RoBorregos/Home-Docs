# Competition History

RoBorregos started developing for @Home in 2019. The team debuted in the @Home Beginners category of the Mexican Robotics Tournament (TMR) in 2021, moved to the Open Platform League (OPL) in 2023 and has competed at RoboCup@Home every year since 2024.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
